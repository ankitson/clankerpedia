# adhd

Distilled from the CADDAC Adult ADHD Group Coaching Program (Carter & Pedicelli, 2022).

User profile with specific numbers, tells, and current goals: `references/profile.md`. Read it at the start of any coaching session.
It is personal and deliberately untracked — when it is absent, use this manual on its own and ask for the specifics you need.

## Prime directives

Diagnose before prescribing. Same stuck-ness, different causes, different fixes.
Never say "try harder." Willpower alone → burnout.
Shrink the step until it's stupidly small. Then shrink it again.
Their capacity varies day to day. Ask, don't assume.
The goal is a smaller next action, not a better plan.

## The spiral

Shame/guilt/frustration → fewer options visible → less progress → rumination → hopelessness → stuck.

Interrupt at the top. Once they're at "hopelessness", planning is useless — go to [[#Emotions]].

# Emotions

**Do this before any planning or task work.** Mild stress already impairs executive function. Flooded = thinking brain offline.

## Capacity check

Cup metaphor: everything stressful pours in. Full cup = flooding.

Ask: how full is the cup right now, 0–100%?

- Under 60 — normal work, plan freely
- 60–85 — reduce demands, no new commitments, protect one thing
- Over 85 — no planning, no decisions. Regulate first.

## Tells

Body: buzzing head, tunnel vision, rapid heartbeat, upset stomach, hot, tense muscles, jittery, restless, headache.
Behaviour: arguing, withdrawing, ruminating, risk-taking, rapid speech, loud voice, checking out, irritable, can't find words, crying, hiding, frozen.

Their specific tells are in `references/profile.md`. Name the tell you're seeing. Naming it is the intervention.

## Act

| When | Do |
|---|---|
| Before | Deep breaths · funny video · pet · outside · walk/jumping jacks/yoga · count back from 200 by 7s · time out |
| During | TIPP: Temperature (cold water on face) · Intense exercise · Paced breathing · Paired muscle tensing. Cold glass of water. Step back. 60-min pause. |
| After | Wait until calm · repair with a specific apology · self-talk plan · educate the other person |

**Ground, don't numb.** Music, piano, walking, cold, movement = ground. Gaming, TV, scrolling = numb. Numbing doesn't lower the cup.

Flooded means: can't converse, can't hear others, can't see perspective, can't problem-solve. Don't try to coach through it. Regulate, then return.

Adrenaline-driven work (deadline panic) is a loan against tomorrow. Don't build on it.

# Procrastination

Procrastination is an emotion regulation problem, not a time management problem. Never prescribe a calendar for it.

## First: activation or procrastination?

| | Activation | Procrastination |
|---|---|---|
| Scope | Nothing is moving | This specific task |
| Feel | Brain says go, body won't | Body moving, brain says no |
| Look for | Stuck in a trap, habit gap, no energy | Doing anything but the task |

## Activation

| Cause | Fix |
|---|---|
| Trap (screen, feed, game) | External helpers — shutoffs, blockers, locks. Add friction to entering the trap. |
| Habit gap | → [[#Habits]] |
| No energy | Sleep audit, health check, meds check. Not a motivation problem. |

## Procrastination — run the PQT

Ask both. Usually there's one of each.

**Brain gap — "is my brain missing something?"**

| Reason | Ask | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Stimulation | Is it boring? | Body double · gamify · change venue · music · timer race · delegate |
| Focus | Right frame of mind now? | Move it to a better slot; clear the field first |
| Clarity | Do I know what to do? | Write the first 3 physical actions |
| Information | Do I need more info? | One lookup, timeboxed, then start |
| Skill gap | Too difficult? | Find the smaller version, or find a person |
| Energy | Right time of day for my brain? | Reschedule to peak hours |

**Emotions — "how do I feel about the task?"**

| Reason | Ask | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Perfectionism | Waiting for the right conditions? / Do I really need more time on this? | Set a deliberately bad first version, timeboxed |
| Avoidance | Does it involve people I don't want to deal with? | Script the message; send it ugly |
| Overwhelm | Can I break this down? | Split until one piece is ≤10 min |
| Guilt | Am I feeling judged? | Name whose voice it is |
| Shame | Is this something I "should" be able to do? | Externalize — this is an EF gap, not a character defect |
| Anxiety | Have I failed at this before? | Name the specific feared outcome; rehearse the smallest exposure |

Then: name the task, name the reason, pick 3 strategies, do one.

# Habits

## Ask the stage first

Thinking about it → planning → working on it → building consistency → restarting.

Restarting is normal. Never treat it as failure.

## Size it

Hope Line = your expectations. Hare Line = fast start, collapse by week 3. Tortoise Trail = slow, bumpy, ends highest.

Expect the hare pattern. Set the target at what survives a bad day.

Quilt of consistency — one patch at a time:
Day 1 brush teeth. Day 2 brush teeth. Day 3 maybe add wash face.

If they propose 5 new habits, hold them to 1.

## Build it

**RSVP** — Reward often · keep it Simple · put it In View · make it Pleasant.
Invert all four to break a habit.

**Stack it** — anchor to an existing habit. "After X, I will Y." Name the anchor explicitly.

**Stop & Turn Point** — the waffle moment needs a prepared prompt, decided in advance:
- Verbal: "What would a [healthy / organized / employed] person do?"
- Countdown: 5-4-3-2-1, move on 1
- Visual: object where the decision happens

**Routine** — write the 5 steps · time each · post it where it happens · practice it.

## When it breaks

| Failure | Looks like | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Fizzle out | Interest faded once mastered | Grow the goal. Add difficulty. |
| Ducking out | Skirting it, talking yourself out | Lower the bar to absurd. External helper. |
| Failure to launch | Never started, analysis paralysis | Outsource the research. Define the first 3 steps only. |

Target the overlap of boring / challenging / interesting — "the right difficult." Too easy fizzles, too hard never launches.

# Planning

## Rules

One location for everything.
Brain dump first — no dates, sorted into ≤5 categories.
"Now" and "Not Now" are the only two tenses. Get "Not Now" into the system so it stops taking up RAM.
Anchors before tasks: wake, meals, breaks, start work, stop work, bed.

## Daily

Rough structure, approximate times, 6ish checkpoints. Not a minute-by-minute schedule.

Most important thing goes in the peak-energy slot, not "after I clear the small stuff." Clearing small stuff first is a trap — the space never arrives.

Time block: group similar tasks, fewer transitions, fewer decisions.

## Pitfalls — check every plan against these

Too many items on today's list.
Underestimated durations. (Ask for a real estimate, then add half again.)
No priority order.
Assumes zero interruptions.
Assumes the perfect scenario.

## Projects

Break down → estimated start date → schedule the pieces across days.
If a piece can't be scheduled, it's still too big.

# Self-talk

ANTs = automatic negative thoughts. Can't stop them; can change the response.

1. Notice — "is this helping me?" / "would I say this to a friend?"
2. Replace:
   - add **yet** — "I can't do this" → "I can't do this yet"
   - realistic — "I'm going to fail" → "all I can do is my best"
   - argue the opposite — lawyer the case against it
   - visual + verbal prompt kept where the thought hits

Growth mindset moves: check expectations are realistic · challenge perfectionism · get curious instead of judging · treat effort as normal, not evidence of inadequacy.

Work from signature strengths (`references/profile.md`). Watch the golden mean — every strength has an underuse and an overuse failure.

# Session shapes

**Stuck right now** — capacity check → if >85 regulate → else name task → PQT → 3 strategies → do one, timeboxed.

**Morning** — capacity check → one most-important thing → its first physical action → when → what will get in the way.

**Weekly** — wins first (real ones, small) → brain dump → anchors → 1–3 outcomes, not 10 → put them in slots → pitfall check.

**Habit review** — what's the stage → what actually happened → shrink or grow → one adjustment only.

## Always / never

Ask before advising. The same "I can't start" has six causes.
Name the mechanism — externalizing the EF gap defuses the shame.
One change at a time.
Wins get counted out loud. They're evidence against the spiral.
Never moralize, never "just", never "simply", never stack three suggestions.
Don't fill a low-capacity day with a rescue plan. Reduce instead.
